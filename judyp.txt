20261020-12.13
1. Use V50 audio HAL from Emanual's tree
2. Bit-perfect Hi-Res audio passthrough & 44.1 kHz family resampling fix 
   - Fixed an issue where the 44.1 kHz audio family was forcibly resampled to 48 kHz family by the HAL layer, even when AudioFlinger reported DIRECT output.
   - Note: Due to SDM845 LPASS hardware clock generator limitations, 352.8 kHz audio is downsampled using an exact 2:1 integer decimation to 176.4 kHz to prevent non-integer SRC harmonic distortion.
   - Exposed kernel sysfs nodes (/sys/kernel/es9218_dac/sample_rate and /sys/kernel/es9218_dac/bit_width) to inspect the active DAC hardware sample rate and bit depth in real time.
3. Excluded standard 48 kHz 16-bit PCM streams from forced DIRECT output routing
4. Fixed an issue in the stock kernel driver where Apodizing fast roll-off filters (types 1 and 2) were hijacked and redirected to Minimum phase fast roll-off
5. Fixed artificial volume attenuation in stock kernel driver (-1 dB on Linear phase slow roll-off and -2 dB on Linear phase fast roll-off)

20261010-12.12
1. Integrated Dolby Atmos support
2. Enable Direct Audio Passthrough Mode (DIRECT Output)
   - When 3.5mm headphones are plugged in and the Hi-Fi Quad DAC is enabled, all music streams are routed directly to the dedicated DAC hardware via DIRECT mode.
   - Recommended to use professional players with Hi-Res/Direct output support, such as Poweramp, UAPP, etc.
   - **Note:** In DIRECT mode, Dolby Atmos will not take effect, and concurrent audio mixing across multiple media apps is unsupported (system notifications and phone calls are unaffected).
3. Redesigned FM Radio UI
4. Quad DAC panel enhancements and fixes
   - Upgraded UI controls and layouts to Material Design 3 card style.
   - Fixed an issue where switching Sound Presets had no effect.
   - Fixed an issue where the L/R channel balance was reset to zero after pausing and resuming playback.
   - Replaced channel balance buttons with vintage-style rotary knobs.
   - Exposed all 8 hardware ROM digital filters supported by the ES9218P DAC (previously limited to 3 in stock and AOSP based ROMs), selectable directly from the list.
   - Standardized digital filter names to official technical terminology: "Short", "Sharp", and "Slow" are now "Apodizing fast roll-off 2", "Linear phase fast roll-off", and "Linear phase slow roll-off", respectively.
5. Updated KernelSU-Next to v3.4.0
6. Fixed loudspeaker audio distortion and clipping at high volume levels
